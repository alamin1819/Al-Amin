About Me

I am passionate about continuously learning new ideas, concepts, and emerging technologies in engineering and sharing my knowledge with students in simple, engaging, and practical ways. I enjoy making learning enjoyable by connecting theoretical concepts with real-life examples and encouraging students to understand not only what they learn, but also why and how it can be applied.

Alongside teaching, I am actively exploring research in energy production and sustainable energy technologies, with the goal of developing knowledge and solutions that can make people’s lives easier and contribute positively to society. Through continuous learning, teaching, research, and innovation, I aspire to create meaningful contributions both nationally and internationally.

Ultimately, my vision is to use my knowledge, skills, and work to serve humanity and contribute to the well-being and development of the global people’s. I believe that continuous learning, purposeful work, and knowledge-sharing are essential pathways toward achieving this vision.
